1
Upcoming / Past tabs
Upcoming shows everything still on the calendar, soonest first. Past is your personal history of everything you attended, RSVP'd to, or missed.
2
Category pill
Rush, Professional, Service, Fundraising, Social, or Meeting — this is what an event's points count toward on your Home standing.
3
RSVP → Cancel RSVP
Tapping RSVP marks you going and swaps the button to Cancel RSVP. It doesn't post points by itself — points post when E-Board (or a QR check-in) records you as attended.
Careful: cancelling inside 24 hours of the event start counts as a late cancel and posts a −10 point penalty. The app warns you and asks you to confirm before it goes through.

07

Check-in (QR)

A fast path to recorded attendance that skips E-Board manually marking every brother present.

Check In
You're checked in
GM x AKPSI x WCG x FMA
+10 pts posted
1
There's no button for this
Check-in isn't a tab you tap into — it only opens from scanning the QR code E-Board projects or posts at an event. Scanning it opens this exact screen, already signed in as you.
2
What it does
Records your attendance and posts that event's points to your Home total immediately — no waiting on E-Board to reconcile a list afterward.
3
If the scan fails
A code only works for its own event and expires after check-in closes. If yours doesn't go through, tell whoever's running the door — they can mark you present manually from the E-Board side.
